Frequently Asked Questions

What songs do you play?

Crown Avenue performs a wide variety of music spanning rock, pop, alternative, country, dance, and other crowd favourites. Visit our Exlore Our Sound page to see examples from our set list.

How far in advance should I book the band?

Booking as early as possible is recommended, especially for weddings, holidays, adn other popular event dates. Availability varies throughout the year.

What is the booking process?

Submit the booking form with your event details and our team will follow up to discuss availability, event needs, pricing, and the next steps required to secure your date.

Do you require a deposit? What do your rates include?

Deposit requirements and rates vary depending on the event, location, performance length, and production needs. We will provide complete details during the booking process.

Can you play my favourite song?

We are happy to discuss special song requests. If there is a particular song you would like performed at your event, include it in your booking request.
