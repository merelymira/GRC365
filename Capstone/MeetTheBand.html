Meet the Band

Bingo

Lead Singer

Meet the Vocal Dynamo of Crown Avenue: Bingo. With a voice that transcends genres and a stage presence that commands attention, Bingo is the heartbeat of our musical journey. Hailing from San Jose, California, his passion for performance has lit up stages worldwide. From soulful ballads to high-energy anthems, Bingo delivers an electrifying experience that captivates audiences and leaves an indelible mark on every performance. Get ready to be enchanted by the vocal prowess and charisma that defines the soul of Crown Avenue, as Bingo takes center stage and transforms every note into a musical masterpiece.
